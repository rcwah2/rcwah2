- **AI Vendor Due Diligence** — Multi-framework vendor assessment covering governance, security, privacy, financial viability, incident response, and agentic AI controls
- **AI System Inventory & Risk Assessment** — Structured documentation and classification of AI systems through a governance lens
- **Enterprise Implementation** — 25+ years delivering complex, regulated systems across healthcare, energy, financial services, and public sector

- [AI Vendor Due Diligence Template](https://github.com/rcwah2/ai-vendor-due-diligence-template) — Three-part governance pack: due diligence questionnaire, SOW/operational requirements, and contract clause checklist.
